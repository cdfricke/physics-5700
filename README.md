## Physics 5700: Advanced Physics Laboratory
### By Connor Fricke, Jayde Spiegel, and Ashley Tarrant
---

This repository contains `data` obtained from experiments performed over the duration of the course. Those experiments are as follows:

1. Michelson Interferometry
2. The Millikan Oil Drop Experiment
3. Young's Double Slit Experiment, One Photon at a Time

In addition, scripts, notebooks, outlines, and reports written by each group member are available in their subdirectories. Commonly used Python modules are available in the `modules` subdirectory.
